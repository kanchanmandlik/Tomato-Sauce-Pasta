🍅 Tomato Sauce Pasta

A Delicious Italian-Style Pasta Recipe

Prep Time

10 Minutes

Cook Time

20 Minutes

Servings

4 People

Ingredients

  • 250g Pasta
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 4 Garlic Cloves (minced)
  • 4 Tomatoes (pureed)
  • 1 tsp Chili Flakes
  • 1 tsp Oregano
  • Salt to taste
  • Black Pepper to taste
  • Fresh Basil Leaves
  • Parmesan Cheese (optional)

Instructions

  1. Boil pasta according to package instructions and drain.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add tomato puree and cook for 8–10 minutes.
  4. Add chili flakes, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  5. Mix in the cooked pasta and toss well.
  6. Cook for 2–3 more minutes.
  7. Garnish with basil leaves and parmesan cheese.
  8. Serve hot and enjoy!
